# Proposal: split SPED:000215 ("Section 504") into separate concepts

Date: 2026-11-23. Status: **applied in 0.53.0** after the maintainer's
decisions (see "Decisions and result" at the end). Drafted by Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`) from the cited
documents. Nine were read from `.sources-cache/` (pdftotext, at the cited
page). Eight have no local copy and were read online at the URL in their layer
module. Those are marked *online*; their page numbers come from the
reading tool and should be confirmed in the PDF.

Quotes are short excerpts (under 15 words) to show the wording; nothing else
from the sources is copied here.

## The problem

SPED:000215 holds five different things under one ID: the **law**, the
**plan document**, the **committee** that writes it, a **complaint**
process, and **Pennsylvania's own rules** (Chapter 15). An agent that looks
up "504 plan" is told it names the statute. Two strings are also not
supported by the document they are cited to (rows 1 and 5 below).

## Proposed records

Concept IDs never change, so SPED:000215 keeps its ID and becomes the law
only. The others are new IDs, each pointing back to it with `broader: s504`,
like the existing 504 Coordinator record.

| Record | Preferred term | Strings (type, region) | Cited by (row #) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SPED:000215** `s504` (kept) | Section 504 | Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act (synonym) | 1, 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11?, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 |
| **new** `s504_plan` | Section 504 plan | 504 plan (synonym); Section 504 Accommodations Plan (synonym, NY wording, replaces the singular form); individual accommodation plan (synonym, US-TX); IAP (acronym, US-TX) | 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 13 |

Strings removed from SPED:000215: 504 plan, Section 504 Accommodation Plan,
Section 504 committee, individual accommodation plan, IAP, Chapter 15,
504 complaint. Each moves to the record above.

## Decisions for you

1. **Preferred term for the plan.** "Section 504 plan" (California; also
   Massachusetts and Iowa) or "504 plan" (the most common form in these
   sources). Either way the other is a synonym. Whether federal law or
   guidance names the plan is worth a check against 34 CFR Part 104. I did
   not check it.
2. **Texas "individual accommodation plan".** It's currently typed
   `related`. The STAAR guide uses it as Texas's name for the plan itself,
   so I propose `synonym` (US-TX). Your call.
3. **"504 hearing" (Illinois).** It's a district hearing, distinct from an
   OCR complaint. Make it its own concept, a string on `s504_complaint`, or
   leave it out for now?
4. **Complaint vs. OCR.** `s504_complaint` could instead be a string on the
   existing "Office for Civil Rights" record. I propose a separate concept,
   because Illinois uses it for both the district process and OCR.
5. **Arkansas (row 11).** The only mention is a training-category name.
   Keep it as a weak attestation, or drop it?
6. **Chapter 15.** The Pennsylvania guide names it but gives no code
   citation. I propose citing only the guide until the regulation itself
   (Pennsylvania Code) is added as a source document. "Service agreement"
   is not in this document, so it isn't proposed.
7. **Row 1 strings.** Texas's notice supports only "Section 504" (via the
   civil-action sentence). "Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act" stays,
   because rows 6, 9, 10, 16 and others have it verbatim. "504 plan" moves to
   the plan record with Massachusetts, New York and Illinois as its sources.

## How it would be implemented (after your decisions)

- `texas.py`: `s504` keeps "Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act"; drop
  "504 plan".
- The plan concept is created in `california.py` (first layer whose source
  uses the preferred wording; it applies after the Texas layers). Texas's
  IAP strings and the committee go on later layers or the Texas
  supplement, because a layer can only refer to concepts defined before it.
  The exact placement is mechanical and doesn't change the data.
- `new_york.py`, `illinois.py`, `massachusetts.py`, `iowa.py`,
  `pennsylvania.py`: move each string and attestation to the record in the
  table. Add a page (p. 5 or p. 9) to New York's citation.
- Run `python -m spedlex.build --register` to give the new concepts IDs.
  Then rebuild, test and record the change in CHANGELOG (a patch version:
  corrections to existing concepts).
- Afterward, your row 4 in `queue-2026-11-23.csv` stays a `problem` against
  the old record. Re-check the new records in the next worklist.

## Decisions and result (2026-11-23)

1. Preferred term "Section 504 plan"; "504 plan" is a synonym.
2. Texas "individual accommodation plan" (and "IAP") are synonyms, tagged US-TX.
   The matching row in `sources/name_pair_triage.tsv` now says `keep`.
3. "504 hearing" is left out.
4. "504 complaint" is its own concept.
5. Arkansas's attestation (row 11) is dropped.
6. Chapter 15 is left out entirely (no concept, no string).
7. Row 1: as proposed.

New IDs: SPED:001674 Section 504 committee, SPED:001675 Section 504 plan,
SPED:001676 504 complaint, each `broader` SPED:000215 and `version_added`
0.53.0. SPED:000215 now has 13 citations. New York's citations are "pp. 5, 9"
(plan) and "p. 9" (committee), confirmed online. Texas's IAP strings are on
the plan through `texas_supplement.py`, because `s504_plan` is defined by
`california.py`, which applies after the Texas layers.
