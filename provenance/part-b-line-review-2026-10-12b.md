## How to send corrections

The CSV is the file to edit. The table below is the same content for reading.

1. **Easiest:** open `part-b-line-review-2026-10-12b.csv` in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Fill in **Your decision** and **Your note** for only the rows you disagree with. Leave the rest blank; blank means I accept my suggestion. Save it, then tell me it is ready.
2. **In chat:** list row numbers and the decision, for example `17 keep; 205 retype; 532 fix: use X`.
3. **Decision codes you can use:**
   - `keep`: same concept; leave it as a name pair.
   - `retype`: related but different; remove it from the yes/no question and record a related-term link instead.
   - `fix`: correct the string. Put the corrected text in **Your note**.
   - `delete`: remove the string entirely.
   - `unsure`: keep it out of the expert email until someone checks.
4. **Add a source when you can.** If your answer depends on a rule or document, put the document and page in **Your note** (for example `8VAC20-81-10, p. 4`). Every entry must cite a document we actually read.
5. **Do not include student information** in any note.
