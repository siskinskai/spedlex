## Notes

- Arkansas has moved its special-education rules into the Code of Arkansas
  Rules as 6 CAR pt. 130. Older copies cite "Special Education and Related
  Services, Section 2.00 Definitions" (2019/2021). Cite the 6 CAR numbering.
- Eligibility detail by category is in DESE's separate "Eligibility Criteria
  and Program Guidelines for Children with Disabilities, Ages 3-21" (2000),
  which DESE now links from its rules. It's worth adding if the Subpart 2
  labels turn out to match the federal ones.
