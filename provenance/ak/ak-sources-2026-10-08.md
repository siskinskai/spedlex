## What we already know (to check against the documents, not to cite)

- 4 AAC 52.130 uses its own category labels: "cognitive impairment",
  "learning disability", "deaf and blind", "early childhood developmental
  delay", alongside the federal ones.
- The definitions are at 4 AAC 52.790, not .990.
- The Parent Training and Information Center is Stone Soup Group.
- `education.alaska.gov/regs/filed/4AAC_52.pdf` is a 2007 amendment packet
  (Register 183), not the compiled chapter. Don't use it as the rule.
- DEED's Special Education Handbook circulates only as district or
  third-party copies; no DEED-hosted copy turned up.
