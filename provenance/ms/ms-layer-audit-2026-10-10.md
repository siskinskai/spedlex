## 3. Summary

- **364 entries: 362 confirmed, 2 misplaced, 0 not found.** 4 needed a hand check. The two misplaced entries are only partly misplaced: entry 263 (`sto`), where two of the four strings are on pp. 32 and 35, and entry 301 (`ms_ose`), whose full name is on rule p. 210.
- **Uncited preferred form:** the `asha` concept's preferred term, "American Speech-Language-Hearing Association", appears in none of the six documents (entry 363). It is the association's real name, but the layer cites only the acronym.
- **Safety:** two new two-letter all-caps acronyms, "DB" (deaf-blindness, rule p. 25) and "LS" (speech or language impairment, rule p. 29), are matched case-sensitively and protected as tokens. IEPs often refer to a student by initials, so a student with the initials D.B. or L.S. written "DB" or "LS" would be shielded from redaction. SPEDLex already protects other two-letter forms (ED, LD, MD, OI), so this is a policy question for the maintainer, not an error in the draft. "ASHA" matches the first name Asha and was added to the known all-caps list at the contributor's instruction; a reviewer should confirm it. "MOM" (method of measurement) is protected as a token; it is not a name but will match the word "MOM" in all-caps text.
- **Typing:** acronyms are typed `A`; Mississippi-only strings carry US-MS. "Itinerate Services" (a misspelling in the guidance) is a variant, which is right.
- **Manifest:** all six `doc_id`s have US-MS rows; `ms-family-guide-mdr-2025` is declared in `UNCITED_DOCS`. No source file or long extract is in the change. The extracted `.txt` files are in `.sources-cache/ms/`, which git ignores.
- **Docstring:** accurate on titles, dates and page counts. The branch's CHANGELOG coverage totals (40 added, 16 pending) were one state short; with all three layers merged it is 41 added, 15 pending.

**Most important fixes**

1. Add the documents' own names for ASHA, and decide whether to keep the uncited preferred form.
2. Add ESEA and the Social Security and vocational-rehabilitation transition terms (SSI, SSDI, SSA, PASS, IPE), which no layer had.
3. Decide on the policy for two-letter all-caps acronyms (DB, LS) before release.

**Update (2026-10-10):** Fixes 1 and 2, the other additions in the table above the "low priority" rows (except DVRS and "ELA") and the two citation corrections are applied on branch `integrate-ky-me-ms`. Fix 3 and the ASHA and ELA all-caps decisions are left for the maintainer.
