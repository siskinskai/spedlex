## Notes

- Iowa's 3-21 rule was revised after the 2023 Red Tape Review (Executive
  Order 10). Use the current IAC chapter (12/10/25), not older copies of
  ch. 41 hosted by AEAs or districts, and cite its rule numbering.
- Do not use the Part C (Early ACCESS, birth to three) safeguards manual
  (July 2026, media/11822) as the PSN; it covers a different program. Early
  ACCESS terms can come from the 3-21 documents where they appear.
- Expect place-name and AEA-specific terms (the nine Area Education
  Agencies are named for regions, e.g. Heartland, Grant Wood, Mississippi
  Bend). Only the generic "Area Education Agency" / "AEA" should be
  considered; individual AEA names are local agencies and are excluded.
