## Open question

ALSDE still calls the Nov. 2013 chapter "the current Alabama Administrative
Code". The legislature's code site
(https://admincode.legislature.state.al.us, chapter 290-8-9) is the official
text. Its history notes for .00-.02 end in 2011, but the notes for .03
onward couldn't be read here. Before drafting, check whether any rule in the
chapter was amended after Nov. 2013. If one was, use the legislature's text
for that rule.

- Chapter 290-8-9-.12 covers gifted, and .11 a "case manager" for children
