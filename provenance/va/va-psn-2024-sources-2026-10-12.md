## How it was read

Extracted with `pdftotext -layout` and read in full. Every record's string was then looked up by a
script on the page it cites (75 records; 0 misses). The notice is the federal model notice with
Virginia additions, so most records only attest existing concepts.

## What changed

- 64 existing concepts now attested for Virginia (federal terms such as mediation, independent educational evaluation, manifestation determination, and the discipline definitions). Three carry a new Virginia-tagged string: "request for due process", "wards of the State", "scholastic records" / "scholastic record".
- 6 concepts the first pass cited to the 2013 Town Hall copy or the family guide are re-attested by the 2024 edition: the Department (cover), the Virginia Regulations (cover), the Office of Dispute Resolution and Administrative Services (cover), PEATC (p. 1), the Parent Ombudsman (p. 1), the State Special Education Advisory Committee (p. 29). The Advanced Studies Diploma is attested at p. 7.
- 4 new concepts, all `candidate`: Complaint Appeal Reviewer (p. 21), IEP Coordinator (p. 7), Parent Resource Center (cover, p. 1), and the title of Board of Education regulation 8 VAC 20-150-10, "Management of the Student's Scholastic Record in the Public Schools of Virginia" (pp. 11-12).
- The note on the Modified Standard Diploma (in `virginia.py`) now says the 2024 notice, p. 7, names only the standard and advanced studies diplomas.

## Things to check

- **Modified Standard Diploma.** The word "modified" does not appear anywhere in the 2024 notice. Where it lists the diplomas a student can graduate with (p. 7: consent is required for ending services "except if your child graduates with a standard or advanced studies diploma"), it names only those two. That agrees with the existing note ("discontinued ... still appears in older records") but does not prove it. The note was not changed beyond adding the page.
- **Standard high school diploma** is not attested from this notice: the words are "standard or advanced studies diploma", not an exact match for any string.
- **"Parent Resource Center"** is read from "your local school division's Parent Resource Center" (cover) and "your local Parent Resource Center" (p. 1). It is kept apart from the existing Community Parent Resource Center concept because the notice does not say they are the same thing.
- **"Qualified examiner"** (a New Hampshire-tagged concept) is attested because p. 9 defines an independent educational evaluation as one conducted by "a qualified examiner". It is a general idea; the maintainers may drop the single-state tag.
- **Flagged high risk by the build, as expected:** the regulation title (contains "Virginia", a first name and a place). It is protected only as a full phrase.
- Left out on purpose: see the module docstring.
