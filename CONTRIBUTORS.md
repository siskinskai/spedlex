# Contributors

Thank you to the special-education professionals who checked SPEDLex entries.
Everyone here **opted in**. People who asked to stay anonymous are not listed;
their reviews still count in `sources/reviews.tsv`.

This file is edited by hand by a maintainer. The expert-review tool
(`tools/expert_review.py reply`) drafts each row from the person's reply and
shows it only if they chose to be named. Their affiliation appears here only
if they chose that, and never in `sources/reviews.tsv`.
